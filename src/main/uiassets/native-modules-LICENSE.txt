Native Module Icons — original artwork for Native Client

The 23 module icons and their font/SVG source were drawn specifically for Native
Client. No Lucide, Feather, or other third-party icon glyphs or paths were used.

These assets are dedicated to the public domain under CC0 1.0 Universal, matching
this repository's LICENSE. See https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/.

Editable artwork and deterministic build: scripts/native-module-icons.py
Codepoint and module mapping: native-modules.json
